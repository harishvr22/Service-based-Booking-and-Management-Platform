ApartmentCare

Apartment Service Management System

A streamlined platform connecting apartment residents with verified service providers. Book maintenance services, track requests, and manage your apartment needs — all in one place.

Key Features

Apartment-Focused

Designed specifically for residential complexes and apartment communities

Verified Providers

All service providers are verified and approved by administrators

Booking Tracking

Real-time status updates from booking to service completion

Admin Security

Centralized admin control for user and provider management

→

→

→

Job done & tracked

User Roles

Resident

Apartment residents can browse available services, book appointments, and track the status of their service requests in real-time.

Service Provider

Verified professionals receive booking requests, accept or decline jobs, and update service status as they complete work.

Admin

System administrators manage users, verify service providers, oversee bookings, and ensure platform security.
